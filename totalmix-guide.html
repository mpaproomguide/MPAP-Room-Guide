TotalMix Guide

Learn how to use TotalMix effectively for audio mixing and routing in the Research Lab.

What is TotalMix?

TotalMix is RME's powerful audio mixing software that provides comprehensive control over audio routing, monitoring, and effects processing. It's essential for managing audio in professional recording and research environments.

Key Features

  • Real-time audio mixing and routing
  • Multiple input and output channels
  • Built-in effects and EQ
  • Low-latency monitoring
  • Snapshot and workspace management
  • Matrix routing capabilities

Basic Navigation

  • Input Channels: Control incoming audio signals
  • Playback Channels: Manage computer audio playback
  • Output Channels: Route audio to speakers and headphones
  • Matrix View: Visual routing grid for complex setups
