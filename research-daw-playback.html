DAW Playback

Digital Audio Workstation Setup

  • Open your Digital Audio Workstation (Pro Tools, Logic Pro, Ableton Live, etc.).
  • In your DAW's audio preferences, set the audio interface to MADIFace USB.
  • MADI 1-64 will now appear in your track inputs and outputs. Outputs 1-23 correspond directly to speakers 1-23, and outputs 25/26 correspond to the Headphones. The other channels are not in use.

Pro Tools

1. In Pro Tools, you may need to default the I/O. Go to Setup > I/O.

2. In Input, Output and Bus, select Default on the bottom of the I/O Window.
