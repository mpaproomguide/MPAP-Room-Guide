DAW Playback

Digital Audio Workstation Setup

  • Open your Digital Audio Workstation (Pro Tools, Logic Pro, Ableton Live, etc.).
  • In your DAW's audio preferences, set the audio interface to MADIFace USB.
  • Configure your project's audio settings to match your desired channel configuration (stereo, 5.1, 7.1, etc.).
  • Set up your track routing to output to the appropriate MADI channels for your speaker configuration.
  • Ensure TotalMix is properly configured to route the MADI outputs to your speakers.
  • Test your setup by playing back audio and verifying it comes through the correct speakers.
